# Codeforces Platform & Contest Guide

*Reference: [How to use Codeforces [GUIDE] by SlavicG](https://codeforces.com/blog/entry/99660) & [After a Round FAQ by djm03178](https://codeforces.com/blog/entry/103654)*

Codeforces is the premier platform for competitive programming worldwide. Understanding its structure and scoring system is essential for steady ranking progression.

## 2. Scoring Systems

### A. Standard Codeforces Rounds
- Problems start with point values (e.g., 500 – 1000 – 1500 – 2000 – 2500).
- **Time Decay:** The points you receive decrease every minute after the contest starts.
- **Penalty for Wrong Submissions:** Each rejected submission (WA, TLE, MLE) deducts **50 points** upon eventual acceptance.

### B. ICPC Style (Div 3 / Div 4 / Educational sometimes)
- Solved problems score 1 point each.
- Ranking is determined by:
  1. Most problems solved.
  2. Lowest total penalty time (time taken from start + 20 or 10 minutes per wrong submission).

## 3. Pretests, System Tests, and Hacking

- **Pretests Passed:** During a contest, your submission is tested against a smaller subset of test cases ("pretests").
- **Hacking Phase:**
  - If you lock a problem (giving up the right to resubmit), you can inspect solutions written by competitors in your "room".
  - If you find a flaw, boundary failure, or unoptimized complexity in their code, you can supply a custom test case to "hack" it.
  - Successful hack: **+100 points**. Unsuccessful hack: **-50 points**.
- **System Testing:**
  - At the end of the round, all passing submissions are re-evaluated against the complete, exhaustive test suite (including all successful hacks created by participants during the contest).
  - If your solution fails any test here, it fails completely (**Wrong Answer on System Test**).
